Visits per family fell sharply in 2025–26

Families served grew from 27 to 34, and staffing grew from 3 to 5 part-time parent educators — but total personal visits dropped from 368 to 144. Average visits per family fell from roughly 13–14 to about 4. Worth checking whether this reflects a real service gap, a change in caseload timing, or a reporting/export issue.
